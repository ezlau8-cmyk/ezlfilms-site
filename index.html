Flintridge Prep · October 2026

Independent Schools Showcase

Flintridge Prep co-hosted an event bringing families and representatives from more than 20 schools together for admissions and financial-aid conversations. The communications team needed a film people would actually watch — not just post.

I wrote the treatment, shot the event, and cut the final film. It ran on the school's official channels and in their admissions outreach, with my name credited on the publish.
